# Condensation Between Window Panes: What It Means and What It Costs to Fix in Edmonton

Condensation between window panes means the seal around your sealed glass unit has failed. Moist air has leaked into the space between the panes, and it can't be wiped away because it's trapped inside. The good news: in most cases you don't need a new window. You can replace just the glass unit, which in Edmonton starts at $399 with YEG Custom Windows.

## What a foggy window actually means

Modern double- and triple-pane windows use a sealed glass unit (also called an insulated glass unit, or IGU). Two or three panes are separated by a spacer and sealed around the edges. The gap is filled with dry air or an insulating gas such as argon.

When that edge seal breaks down, three things happen:

1. Humid air gets into the gap and condenses on the inside surfaces of the glass.
2. The insulating gas leaks out, so the window loses much of its insulating value.
3. Over time, minerals in that moisture leave a permanent cloudy film or streaks on the glass.

You'll notice it most on cold mornings or when the sun hits the window. Some days it looks fine, other days it's foggy. That on-and-off pattern is a classic sign of a failed seal.

## Why window seals fail

- **Age.** Seals wear out. Many units start failing after 10 to 20 years.
- **Edmonton's temperature swings.** Glass and frames expand and contract between -30°C winters and +30°C summers, stressing the seal every year.
- **Direct sun.** South- and west-facing windows heat up more and tend to fail first.
- **Poor installation or drainage.** Water sitting against the bottom edge of the glass breaks down the seal.
- **Pressure washing or harsh cleaners** aimed at the edges.

## Foggy between the panes vs frost on the inside

If you can wipe the moisture away from inside the room, it's surface condensation, usually caused by indoor humidity. If you can't touch it because it's between the glass, the seal has failed. (See our guide to [frost on the inside of windows](/frost-inside-windows-edmonton) for the humidity side of the problem.)

| Replace the sealed glass unit | A new glass unit is made to size and installed in your existing frame | Frame is solid; only the glass is foggy | Yes — a new sealed unit |

## FAQ

### Can you fix a foggy window without replacing the whole window?

Yes. If the frame is in good condition, only the sealed glass unit needs replacing. This is usually much cheaper than a full window.

### Is condensation between window panes a problem?

Yes. It means the seal has failed and the insulating gas has escaped, so the window is losing heat. Left alone, the glass can become permanently cloudy.

### Does window defogging work?

Defogging can clear the view for a while, but it doesn't reseal the unit or replace the insulating gas. Replacing the glass unit is the lasting fix.

### Is a broken window seal covered by warranty?

Many manufacturers warranty sealed units for a number of years. Check your paperwork or ask us to look for the manufacturer's stamp on the spacer bar.
